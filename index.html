For parents and tutors

A short session. Something useful to talk about.

Sit together for a few puzzles. Ask your learner to explain why they chose a move, and tell us where the picture or instruction left them unsure.

Learn without a connection

The installed lessons work offline. Learners do not need an email address or account.
