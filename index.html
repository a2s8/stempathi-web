STEMpathi at a glance

What it is
A hands-on math app. Children move cubes, tens and hundreds on a balance scale and see why each step works.
Ages
Made for ages 5 to 11, from first counting (Pre-K–K) to equations (grades 5–7).
What it teaches
Counting, more and fewer, making ten, tens and ones, hundreds, adding and taking away with place value, equal groups, and equations with an unknown x.
Price
Free to download. Today every lesson is free and there are no in-app purchases.
Privacy
No account, no ads, no analytics, no servers of ours. The app sends nothing to us; progress stays on the device (and in your own Google backup if it is on).
Internet
Works fully offline after install.
Platform
Android, in a Google Play test (an adult requests access). iPhone and iPad: not yet available.
Maker
Sripathi.One, a small independent studio.

Built to earn your trust

No ads. No account. No servers of ours.

We built STEMpathi the way we want our own children's apps built. Here is what that means, and how to check it.

No ads

None in the app, and no ad code inside it. Nothing to sell your child's attention to.

No account

No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and start.

No servers of ours

STEMpathi has no backend. Nothing about your child is sent to us. Progress stays on the phone, and in your own Google backup if you turned that on.

No tracking

No analytics, no tracking code, no third-party SDKs watching play.

Works in airplane mode

Every lesson runs offline. Try it: switch on airplane mode and play.

Nothing to wander into

No chat, no social feed, no links for the child. Settings and links sit in the grown-ups area, behind a gate.

Honest about money

Today every lesson is free. From version 1.0 it will be free to start, with one optional family unlock bought once. No subscription, and your child never sees a price.

Real people

A small independent studio. Email us and a person replies.

Check it yourself: play with airplane mode on, and read our privacy policy.

Later on the journey

The same balance finds what x is worth.

When counting and place value are solid, the balance turns into algebra. Move blocks, take away matching amounts from both pans, and discover what x is worth.

For parents and tutors

A short session. Something useful to talk about.

Sit together for a few puzzles. Ask your learner to explain why they chose a move, and tell us where the picture or instruction left them unsure.

Learn without a connection

The installed lessons work offline. Learners do not need an email address or account.
