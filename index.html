Join the Android beta in three steps.

Complete these steps in order. Use the same Google account for your email request, tester opt-in and Play Store download.

  1. 1

    Send us your Google-account email

    Email r2k8@sripathi.one with the address you use for Google Play. We will add it to the tester list and confirm when it is ready.

    1. Request access

    Wait for our confirmation before step 2. Use an adult's account; do not send a child's email or personal details.

  2. 2

    Become a tester

    After we confirm your address is on the list, open the opt-in page. Sign in with that Google account and choose Become a tester.

    2. Become a tester ↗
  3. 3

    Download from Google Play

    After opting in, tap Download it on Google Play on the confirmation page, or use the link below. Check that the Play Store uses the same Google account, then tap Install.

    3. Open Google Play ↗

The closed test is free. If Google says the app is unavailable, check the signed-in account and complete step 2 first. Access can take a little time to update. If it still does not work, contact us.
