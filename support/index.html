STEMpathi support

STEMpathi: Equation Explorer is a visual algebra app for kids. It works offline, has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and keeps progress on your device.

Contact

Email us and we answer within a few days. Please include your phone model, Android version and the app version shown in Settings.

r2k8@sripathi.one

Common questions

Progress disappeared? Progress is stored only on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it; updating the app does not.

Something looks wrong on my phone? Tell us the chapter and what you tapped; a screenshot helps.

A maths answer seems wrong? Please send the exact equation shown at the top of the lesson.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy. We never ask for a child's name or any personal details.
