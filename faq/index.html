What is STEMpathi?

STEMpathi: Hands-on Math is a learning app where children move cubes, tens and hundreds on a balance scale. They count, make ten, build numbers with place value and solve equations by keeping both pans equal.

What ages is it for?

It is made for ages 5 to 11. It starts with first counting (Pre-K–K) and goes on to equations (grades 5–7), so a family can use it for several years.

Is there a good math app for a 5 year old that works offline?

STEMpathi works fully offline after install and starts at Counting Shore, the first island for the youngest learners: quick looks at small groups, counting, more and fewer, and keeping a balance level. It has no ads and no account.

Does STEMpathi have ads?

No. There are no ads of any kind in the app.

Is STEMpathi free?

Yes, it is free to download. Today every lesson is free and there are no in-app purchases.

Does my child need an account or an email?

No. There is no login. A grown-up sets up a learner with an animal picture and a fun name; we suggest a nickname, not a full name.

What data does the app collect?

None for us. There are no analytics and no ads. Learner profiles and progress stay on the device, and in the phone's own Google backup if it is turned on.

What does a hands-on balance teach that a worksheet does not?

It shows why. When a child takes 3 cubes from each pan of x + 3 = 8 and the scale stays level, they see that both sides stay equal and that x is 5. The equal sign becomes 'same value on both sides', not 'the answer comes next'.

Does it follow the school curriculum?

The islands practise topics from the US Common Core standards, from kindergarten counting (K.CC) to grade 6 equations (6.EE). The full table is on the How it teaches page. This is our own alignment, not an endorsement.

Which devices does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, through a Google Play test that an adult joins. It is not yet available for iPhone or iPad.

How long is a session?

Each island is a short set of puzzles. Ten to fifteen minutes with a grown-up nearby is a good start, and the app has no timers or streak pressure.

Who makes STEMpathi?

Sripathi.One, a small independent studio that makes calm learning apps for children. Contact: r2k8@sripathi.one.
