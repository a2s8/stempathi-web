Thank you for testing STEMpathi.

Thank you for testing STEMpathi: Equation Explorer, a hands-on algebra app for children aged 6 to 12. The test build is free. It works offline and keeps everything on your phone. Twenty minutes is plenty.

Join

  1. On the phone that will be used, sign in to Google Play with the email address you gave us.
  2. Open the invitation link we sent you and tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play".
  3. Install. The app may show a temporary technical name until Google finishes its review; that is normal.
  4. Stay in the test for 14 days (do not leave the programme) even if you only open the app a few times; Google counts opted-in days.

What to try (with a child if you can, or on your own)

  • Welcome → Start learning → the hands-on introduction (drag blocks onto the balance).
  • Chapter 1: solve two or three puzzles. Use Undo once. Tap Hint once.
  • Journey: spin the globe, tap an island, go back.
  • Chapter 7: exchange a ten for ten ones.
  • Settings: switch night mode on and off; turn calm motion on.
  • Turn the phone sideways on one lesson.

What to tell us

Email r2k8@sripathi.one or use the Play "Send feedback to the developer" link. Most useful:

  1. Phone model and Android version (Settings → About phone).
  2. What you tapped, what you expected, what happened. A screenshot helps.
  3. Anything a child found confusing or boring, in their words.
  4. Any maths that looked wrong: send the equation shown at the top of the lesson.

Please do not send

A child's name, age, photo, school, or a recording of them. We do not want it and the app never asks for it.

Privacy

The app collects nothing. No account, no ads, no analytics. Policy: https://stempathi.sripathi.one/privacy/ · Support: https://stempathi.sripathi.one/support/
