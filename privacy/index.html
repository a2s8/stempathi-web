STEMpathi: Equation Explorer: privacy policy

Updated October 26, 2026 · This is the same text shown inside the app under Settings › Privacy policy.

In one sentence

The app collects nothing: no name, no email, no account, no analytics, no ads. Progress stays on your device and is deleted when you clear the app's storage.

About This App

STEMpathi: Equation Explorer is provided under the Sripathi.One developer brand. This policy describes the current offline app, not unrelated websites or future services. Privacy and support contact: r2k8@sripathi.one.

Information On Your Device

The app stores optional age-band and math-experience choices, hint preferences, appearance/accessibility settings, puzzle progress, learning-day streaks, explanation-check results and unfinished puzzle state. These records are used for local recommendations, progress and recovery. The app does not ask for a learner's name, exact birthday, email address or account. Age-band selection is optional and is not identity verification.

Collection And Sharing

The current app has no learner login, advertising, analytics, social posting, in-app purchases or runtime AI service. It does not transmit learner profiles, answers or progress to Sripathi.One or an app-operated server. It does not request access to contacts, location, microphone or camera. We do not sell learner data.

Platform Services

Google Play and the device operating system may process downloads, security checks, diagnostics or store information under their own policies. The inspected Android candidate disables Android app backup. Device transfer, manufacturer tools and future platform behavior can differ; platform services are not operated by Sripathi.One.

Retention And Deletion

Local records remain until deleted from the device. Learner setup can be erased in the app's learner setup screen; this does not erase all puzzle progress. To remove all local app records on Android, use system Settings > Apps > STEMpathi > Storage > Clear storage (labels vary by device). This permanently removes local preferences and progress. Uninstalling ordinarily removes app-local records, subject to device/platform retention behavior. There is no app-operated server account or learner database to delete.

Children And Families

The app is intended for learning, including younger learners, with local data minimization. It does not offer child chat, public profiles or online sharing. Adults should handle support requests and device-level deletion. Do not send children's names, faces, account details or private school information in feedback.

Support Email

If you email r2k8@sripathi.one, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send. Use a device/build description and reproducible steps instead of sensitive learner information. Support correspondence is separate from offline gameplay. It is used to respond, troubleshoot and maintain necessary support records, and may be processed by our email provider. You may request deletion of your support correspondence; necessary legal/security records may be retained where required.

Security And Changes

The app relies on device access controls for local records. No storage method is completely secure. New cloud, account, telemetry, payment or AI features will require an updated policy and appropriate safeguards before release. The current policy is available offline from Settings > Privacy policy.
