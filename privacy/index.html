Privacy

STEMpathi: Hands-on Math: privacy policy

Updated November 23, 2026 · This is the same text shown inside the app under Settings › Privacy policy.

In one sentence

The app collects nothing for us: no account, no email, no ads, no analytics. Learner profiles and progress stay on the device, and in the phone's own Google backup if it is turned on.

What the app keeps on the device

  • Up to four learner profiles. Each one has:
    • an animal picture and a fun name made from it (for example "Bouncy Bunny");
    • an age group (such as 5–6 or 7–8), never a birthday;
    • a starting level;
    • hint and progress records.
  • A grown-up can change a learner's name in the grown-ups area. We suggest a nickname, not a full name.
  • Settings such as sound, night mode and calm motion.

What we never collect

  • We have no learner login, ads, analytics, social posting or chat, and the app's own code never connects to the internet. The optional one-time unlock is bought through Google Play or the App Store, which use their own connection, and we never see payment details.
  • The app does not send names, answers or progress to us or to any server we run.
  • It does not ask for contacts, location, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell data.

Android backup

  • Android can back up app data to the Google account on the phone, and restore it when the app is installed again or moved to a new phone.
  • STEMpathi allows this only for learner profiles, progress and settings.
  • This backup is run by Google under Google's privacy policy. We never receive it.
  • To turn it off, go to the phone's Settings › Google › Backup (the wording varies by phone).

Deleting data

  • Grown-ups area › Learners › Remove deletes that learner and their progress.
  • To delete everything, go to Settings › Apps › STEMpathi › Storage › Clear storage.
  • Uninstalling also removes the data from the phone. A Google backup may stay in your Google account until you delete it there.

Children and families

  • The app is made for children aged 5–11, with grown-ups in charge of the settings.
  • It has no chat, public profiles or sharing.
  • Please don't send children's names, photos or school details to support.

Support email

  • If you write to r2k8@sripathi.one, we receive your address, your message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • We use them only to help you. You can ask us to delete them.

Changes

  • Any new feature that sends data (accounts, payments, cloud) will need a new policy before it ships.
  • This policy is also inside the app under Settings › Privacy policy.
